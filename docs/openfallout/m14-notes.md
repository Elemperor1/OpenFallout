# M14 notes (move and fight)

What the M14 pull requests changed, and what they rest on. The exe reads are in [m13-exe-reads.md](m13-exe-reads.md) and the M12 notes; nothing from the games is copied here.

## Pad menus (PR 159)

The first hour of play with an Xbox pad (round 13X) found the menus clumsy and weapon equipping "broken". The cause was one thing: the engine's A is a click at the stick-driven pointer (`mwinput/controllermanager.cpp`), while the D-pad moved a pick inside the Lua screens, so A never acted on the pick, and X in the Pip-Boy is Drop (as in the games' own `inventory_menu.xml` hints: A Equip, X Drop, Y Repair, RB Hotkey, LB Mod, B Cancel).

- The pad follows the last device used (the games, on a pad: the cursor is hidden and the screen picks by selection, A acts on the selection). A button that moves the pick makes the screen hold it (`input._setPadPick`); while it is held the engine does not click at the pointer and `falloutpad.translate` gives A as Enter. The stick moving the pointer, or the end of the screens, gives A back to the pointer.
- The triggers are axes; a screen hears them as the buttons `LeftTrigger` and `RightTrigger` (`input.CONTROLLER_BUTTON`, 100 and 101) while a screen is open.
- Pip-Boy: LB/RB pages, D-pad sides tabs, LT/RT list pages, A use, X drop, Y repair. The tab buttons are a choice of this port; the games' files do not say.
- The engine logs keys, buttons and sticks as `Input:` lines (`mwinput/inputlog.*`, limited), and screens log `Pad:` lines.
- Checks: `scripts/openfallout/test_falloutpad.py` (the pick rules, the Pip-Boy map, the screens that must use `translate` or release the pick). The arena smoke test has a chest step that takes with A after the D-pad; **it needs the menu files of the games to show the container screen, so on the synthetic data of Linux the chest steps (and the lock step before them) fail on master as well** (round of 2026-11-11: the same lines with and without this change). Not tried with a real pad yet.

## Difficulty and the god mode

- The difficulty of the game scales the damage of a blow to health when the player is on one end of it (`FalloutScript::FallDamage::blowMultiplier`: fDiffMultHPToPCVE/E/N/H/VH = 0.5/0.75/1/1.5/2 for what the player takes, fDiffMultHPByPCVE/E/N/H/VH = 2/1.5/1/0.75/0.5 for what the player does; the helpers of both executables, see the exe reads). `FalloutScripts::strike` applies it to the damage that reaches the health, after the part of the body took its share; the condition of a limb and the wear of armor still go by the damage before it, as a fall does. Blows between two others are as they are. Companions are not counted as the player.
- The console's god mode keeps the health, limbs and armor of the player against a blow (the blow still lands: the one who struck does not think it missed). This is also the way for a test script to hold the player's health: `debug.toggleGodMode()` after `debug.isGodMode()`.

## Rounds 14B to 14E (2026-11-12): what the rounds found, and what was done

- **The Mac panic of 14B** was not the build and not the engine's doing alone: WindowServer's main thread was blocked behind `runningboardd` (stuck in the kernel), launchd killed it and the replacement never started. The rounds now run behind guards (`scripts/openfallout/round/roundguard.py`, README "Guards": one lock, a pre-flight that warns, a WindowServer heartbeat that kills the engine, a kill switch, the accessory activation policy, builds held to 6 jobs).
- **First-person arms** (`FalloutPlayerBody::buildArms`, `placeArms`): the skeleton of `meshes/characters/_1stperson` with the race's `...hand1st` hands (and the Pip-Boy glove) hangs from the camera in the first-person render bin and field of view, shows when a weapon is out, and plays the weapon's equip, aim and attack files. There is no first-person model of a gun: the games draw the same model as in the third person. Its place is the skeleton's `Camera1st` node at the camera, and `OPENFALLOUT_FIRSTPERSON_ARMS="x y z yaw pitch roll"` moves it for tuning (`rounds/m14-arms-tune.json`).
- **A fight against the player ended after half a second** because the scenario named the player `0x14`, as the games do, and the engine's reference of the player has the number of its plugin in front. `core.combat` now takes a plain `0x14` for the player and has `core.combat.player()`.
- **Bodies that were shot dead stood upright:** a character with no package has no mind (the object that runs its fall) until something gives it one, and nothing did when it was struck. `FalloutScripts::wakeMind` makes the mind when a blow starts a fight or kills.
- **V.A.T.S. "No target in sight"** was the round standing inside the Prospector Saloon (round 14D logged the base record of what the ray hit: the building's collision). The test place was moved and the screen now asks about the head, chest and legs. The round steps (`r.place`, `r.vatsInfo`, ...) that made the 14A scenarios are in `scripts/openfallout/round/scripts` with tests.
- **Logs for the next round:** `units apart: stealth ...` (what a watcher makes of the player), `does not attack the player on sight`, `Weapon of ...` (where the weapon in a hand is and which way it points, a second after it was taken out and when the gait changes).
- **Third person, seen in 14D/14E:** the pistol is on the hand, held flat at the hip pointing forward, and is not seen while the body walks (from behind) or in the crouched idle; the weapon sat on `Bip01 R Hand` without the attach pose (found in 14F: the `Weapon` node is in both skeletons, an empty node that the optimizer took away, see below); the body does not crouch when sneaking.

## Round 14F (2026-11-12): arms and a fight on the Mac, and what came of it

- **The first-person arms are drawn** (hands low at the centre right, small, pistol rolled 90 degrees, no forearm in view), and the log said `Weapon none`. Cause: `Weapon`, the mount that turns the gun's model axes (barrel +X, up +Y) into the hand frame, is an empty node in both skeletons, and `REMOVE_REDUNDANT_NODES` of the optimizer took it away (only the names in `makeSortedReservedNames` of `scenemanager.cpp` are kept: `Weapon Bone` but not `Weapon`). `Resource::isReservedNodeName` now keeps `Weapon` (by prefix) and `ProjectileNode`. The same loss put the long gun of the third-person body pointing at the ground.
- **A fight:** the gangers held the place they stood at 20 m away and shot (rifle: engage distance 1,400 under the clamp of 1,500), and the player's camera ray passed them 26 times of 28 (the scenario now fires at the character itself after a miss, up to 30 shots, so that a kill is seen). **The Powder Gangers are neutral to the player in the data** (their faction has relations to itself only, and `PlayerFaction` names none of them), so `does not attack the player on sight: aggression 1, standing neutral` is right; the game turns them hostile by script. The detection round now calls `core.combat.setEnemy` (`r.enemy`) for the two factions.
- **V.A.T.S. works** on the Mac: it opens, shows the body-part boxes with their chances, queues a shot, fires one and closes (AP 80 to 66, ammunition 17 to 16); the camera and the slow motion of the games are not made.
- **Third person:** the player's body now sneaks, walks backward and sideways and moves with the weapon in its hands: `chooseFalloutMove` picks the file by the prefix of the weapon type (`1hp`, `2ha`, ...), sneaking (`sneakmt*`, with `sneak2hh*` of its own and `sneakmtidle` for standing), the direction (`chooseFalloutDirection` from how the actor moved relative to where it faces) and speed, with the fallbacks that the files need (2ha has no slow walk to the left, sneaking has one set for all). There are no `crouch` files at all: sneaking is the crouch. The creatures keep the forward walk and run they had.
- **Walking where the navigator has no path** (the vault of Fallout 3, a target off the mesh, the pistol raider of round 10O): `falloutStraightWalkable` looks along the line every 50 units for floor (steps of at most 70 units) and a clear way at knee and chest height; `buildPath` uses it for a path that goes nowhere, one the navigator refuses and the end of a path that stops short of its target (`walks straight to X,Y,Z (why)` in the log).

## Round 14G (2026-11-12): the arms, falls, V.A.T.S. and the fight with the Powder Gangers, and what was found

- **First-person arms are right** after the `Weapon` node fix: the hands sit low at the centre right, the pistol is upright (axes X forward, Y up) and the long gun is held upright; the forearms are in view. Third-person pistol and sneaking are right too; **the long gun of the third-person body (`2haaim`) still points down and back**, and rifles of the world's characters point right and up. The pose of a two-handed gun in third person is not understood (the aim file plays on the upper body; the pistol's hip-ready pose looks plausible); it wants a dump of what `2haaim.kf` and `2haidle.kf` move.
- **Third-person movement files** (`Movement of X: file (gait, direction, sneaks, weapon prefix)`) were seen for the player in 14G on the Mac: sneaking plays `sneakmt*` and walking with a pistol `1hp*`.
- **Deaths and falls:** four `dies: its body falls` lines, the bodies lie where they fell; no character is launched into a fall yet.
- **V.A.T.S.** opens, queues and fires (see 14F).
- **Why the gangers did not go for the player:** `does not attack the player on sight` said `its factions` empty for every placed Powder Ganger (the characters of the other cells had theirs). Their records have no factions of their own and take them from a levelled list of characters in the place of a template (`Template_UseFactions`); `templateOwner` in `falloutscripts.cpp` could not follow a list, so `initialFactions` gave none. `initialFactions` and `essentialByRecord` now follow the chain with the seed of the reference (`templateRecordOf`, as the stats do). The detection round calls `r.enemy` for the Powder Gangers' faction and the player's; with factions on the gangers the call has a member to act on. The records alone still say they are neutral to the player.
- **Round 14H (the fix on the Mac):** the placed Powder Gangers now list their factions (GeneralIdle, PowderGangerFactionNV, ArmorPowderGangerFactionNV, vDialoguePowderGangerFaction), and with `r.enemy` set the first one that sees the player `sees the player and goes for it`: it turns, aims and shoots (`2haaim`, `2haattack3`) where it stands. Stealth against a crouched player with Sneak 100 is -33 to -60 at 3 to 30 m (`does not see it`), against the same player standing 17 at 3 m (`sees it`), so the gangers notice a sneaking player only when it stops sneaking. In the 20 m fight the gangers stand still while they shoot, because inside their engage distance (1,400 units for a rifle) the fight AI holds. **Phase F** (a fight that starts 35 m away) shows that they do walk: the ganger ran (`2hafastforward`, then `2haforward`) from 1,846 to 1,407 units in 1.7 s, stopped at the engage distance and shot from there (`2haaim`/`2haattack3` over `mtidle` legs) for the rest of the 17 s, with no `walks straight` line (the navigator's path was used). The figure is too small in the pictures to show its pose.

## M14 part two: hit reactions, falls, effects and the other movements (2026-11-13, New Vegas and Fallout 3)

One change on the Mac, run in hidden rounds (`scripts/openfallout/round/rounds/m14p-*.json`; the scenarios `m14p_reactions.lua`, `m14p_reactions_fo3.lua` and `m14p_swim.lua`). All of it is in `FalloutNV.exe` terms where the executable was read (the knockdown check at 0x646400, the knockback force at 0x646580 and its use at 0x89C8CF, the speed of a character at 0x647D10, the mobility factor at 0x646800), and the numbers are the settings' defaults, which the plugins of both games leave alone.

- **Knockback.** A blow that deals damage throws the character or creature it hits (not the player) away from whoever struck, by the force of `FalloutScript::HitReactions::knockbackForce` (`(agility * 10 * fKnockbackAgilMult + fKnockbackAgilBase) * (damage * fKnockbackDamageMult + fKnockbackDamageBase)`, at most fKnockbackForceMax 512, working down to nothing over fKnockbackTime 1 s). `FalloutActors::knock` moves it a step each frame, stopped by what is in its way (a ray at knee height). Seen: a blow of 13.8 damage moved a Powder Ganger of New Vegas about 60 units in a second, and a raider of Fallout 3 (a weaker blow, 4.3 damage) 55 to 165 units.
- **Crippled legs.** The conditions of the legs are the actor values 0x1D and 0x1E; one crippled leg leaves a character 0.85 of its speed and two leave 0.75 (fMoveOneCrippledLegSpeedMult, fMoveTwoCrippledLegsSpeedMult). The characters of the world walk by it (`FalloutActors`) and the player's own maximum speed has it (`Npc::getMaxSpeed`). A limping character plays the files `<way>_hurt.kf` of the folder `locomotion/hurt` (the weapon's prefix first, else `mt`; none for sneaking, as in the games), on both games. Seen on New Vegas: a ganger with two crippled legs went 0.74 of the distance of one with sound legs in the same time (0.75 expected), and `mtforward_hurt`/`mtfastforward_hurt` played; on Fallout 3 the crippled raider limped (`mtforward_hurt`) and ran at roughly three quarters of the pace of the same raider before its legs were hurt (the other raider of that round walked a patrol, so it is no yardstick).
- **Falls.** A character that is thrown off the edge of the ground (the ground it stands on ends more than a step below) goes on in the air with the speed it had and falls with the gravity of the player (8.96 m/s² times the 69.99 units a metre), and lands on what a ray finds. The landing hurts by the rule of the player's fall (`FallDamage::damage`: fJumpFallHeightMult times the height over fJumpFallHeightMin, to the power 1.65, and the legs take a share of it) and a character with nothing left dies and falls as a body. Seen: 621 units down hurt for 3 points, 812 (New Vegas) and 910 and 1,089 (Fallout 3) killed. A character is only thrown off an edge by a blow; nothing makes an idle character walk off one.
- **Muzzle flash.** A gun whose projectile record has a flash model and a flash duration above 0 shows that model on the `ProjectileNode` of the weapon (the weapon itself when its model names none) for those seconds, in the hands of the world's characters, of the player's body and of the first-person arms. A projectile that says 0 seconds (the silenced guns') has none. Seen in a picture on New Vegas and in the log on both games (flashes of 0.04 s for pistols and rifles, 0.2 s for the flamer of Fallout 3). The model's own controllers run from the moment it is shown (`EffectAnimationTime`, as the effects of spells do).
- **Impacts.** The records `IPCT` and `IPDS` are in the store now. A blow that lands on a character or creature shows the impact that the weapon's impact data set (`INAM` of the weapon) has for the material of the body: the sets list twelve (stone, dirt, grass, glass, metal, wood, organic, cloth, water, hollow metal, organic bug, organic glow); a character is organic, a mutated insect organic bug, a robot metal. The model is put on the node of the part that was hit (the body part data's node for the part of the blow, the torso for a blow at the body) for the seconds the impact record says (0.08 s for blood), turned so that its normal faces the one who struck and a hand's breadth out. Seen in a picture on New Vegas (with `OPENFALLOUT_EFFECT_HOLD=40`, which makes effects play 40 times slower and last 40 times longer so that a picture catches them; the games' own are a fraction of a second) and in the log on both games. **Unverified: the splat is drawn as a bright orange-white blob, where blood should be red; the model uses the no-lighting shader with a grey texture, and the colour it should take is not known.** Shots at things that are not characters (walls, ground) show nothing: the shots are not flown, so there is no hit point and no surface material.
- **Jump, landing, swimming and turning.** The files `<prefix>jumploop<way>`, `<prefix>jumpland<way>`, `swimmt<way>`/`swimmtidle` and `<prefix>turnleft/right` (and the sneaking ones) are used by the player's body and the characters of the world: a character is in the air while it is thrown or falls (told by what moves it), the player's body when it has been off the ground for 0.15 s (the world's `isOnGround`), and the landing plays for 0.4 s; the player's body in water swims (`isSwimming`); a character that stands and turns faster than half a radian a second plays the turn of its direction for as long as it turns and 0.35 s more. Seen: a thrown character on both games played `mtjumploopforward` and `mtjumplandforward`; the player's body, put 700 units up and let go, played `mtjumploop`, then `mtjumpland`, and in the lake `swimmtidle` and `swimmtforward`; characters turned with `1hpturnright` and `mtturnleft`. Not seen: the player's body turning (the round's camera steps do not turn the body), a turn in water.
- **Dead characters** no longer list their movement and attack groups in the sampler (`die()` disables them).

Added without being wired: the knockdown rule (`knockdownKind`, `knockdownPerMille`: 0x646400's thresholds and chance), tested but nothing plays a knockdown or a get-up.

Checks: `testhitreactions.cpp` (the rules), `testfalloutanimation.cpp` (the files chosen for limping, jumping, swimming and turning), and the rounds above (`m14p-reactions` on New Vegas: 0 errors, `m14p-reactions-fo3`: 0 errors, `m14p-swim`, `m14p-effects` for the pictures). Full runs: openfallout-tests 836/836, components-tests 6470 of 6485 (the 15 known failures of NifOsg and `MiscToEulerAnglesZYX` on macOS).
